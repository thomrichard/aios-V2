Welcome to the ring

You just clicked a ball. That is the whole trick: every HTML file your agent ships into the artifacts folder becomes one of these - a permanent, clickable bookmark orbiting the center of your OS.

Three ways this gets useful fast:

  1. Reports find you again. A research brief from three weeks ago is one click, not a folder dig. Press / on the OS to search every ball.
  2. Skill runs self-register. Every headless run from the skills deck leaves its report here automatically.
  3. Your agent keeps the habit. RING.md has a copy-paste snippet for your agent's instructions file - after that, everything it builds lands here without being asked.

This sample (and its two neighbours) are just files in artifacts/ - delete them whenever you like.
